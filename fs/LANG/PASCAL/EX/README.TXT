/LANG/PASCAL/EX -- the Tube demos of /LANG/BBCBASIC/EX, in Mad Pascal.
Each is a port of the .BBC of the same name; CD /LANG/PASCAL/EX then NAME
runs one, and any key leaves it (the BBC ones take Escape).  BBC.PAS is
the unit they share: the Tube ULA's sums in Pascal -- BBC coordinates,
MODE palettes, GCOL, DRAW, CIRCLE FILL, VDU 19, the VDU 23,27 sprites and
SOUND -- so a port draws and sounds as its original does.

KALEID   a kaleidoscope of mirrored lines
CIRCLES  random filled circles (any key leaves)
ROSES    rose curves, a new one every few seconds
CLOCK    an analogue clock on the machine clock
TUNE     Frere Jacques, a round for 3 voices
BOUNCE   a bouncing ball with a blip
MOUNTAIN a scrolling mountain ridge
SPRITES  64 hardware sprites: eight balls drawn once, then VICKY moves them
INVADERS a marching, animated, flipping row of captured sprites
TUNNEL   a palette-cycling tunnel: fifteen circles, no redrawing
TEST     a self-test of the areas TEST.BBC tests, PASS/FAIL per area
