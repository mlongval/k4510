INVADERS -- the 1978 arcade: stop the rack before it lands.

  Left Right   move the cannon         Space   fire (one shot at a time)
  P            pause                   M       sound on / off
  Esc          end the game

THE RACK is five rows of eleven: squids 30 points, crabs 20, octopodes
10.  It steps one invader at a time, as the arcade's did, so the fewer
there are the faster it goes; it drops at the edges, and if one reaches
the cannon's row the game is over.  The beat is the march's own: one of
the four bass notes for every step of the rack.

THE BUNKERS are worn away a pixel at a time -- by the bombs from above,
by your own shots from below, and by invaders marching through them.

THE MYSTERY SHIP crosses the top about every 25 seconds while eight or
more invaders stand: 50 to 300 points, by the arcade's table (the 23rd
shot, and every 15th after it, is worth the 300).  One more cannon at
1500.  Each wave starts lower than the last.

HISCORE.DAT here keeps the five best scores and initials.
