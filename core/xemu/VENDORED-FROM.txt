On the MEGA65 relationship, which is easy to get slightly wrong.  Xemu is
Gábor Lénárt's own project at github.com/lgblgblgb/xemu -- it is not a
MEGA65-project repository, though it is the emulator the MEGA65 community
uses and he is a MEGA65 contributor.  What is genuinely MEGA65 here is
what the code emulates: cpu65.c describes itself as a 65C02 / 65CE02 "as
the form used in 4510 in C65, also with its extension in the MEGA65", and
credits lydon on the MEGA65 Discord for work on the Q-opcodes in the
mega65-core VHDL.

The cycle table is a THIRD upstream, not Xemu: cpu65_mega65_timings.h was
generated by LGB's separate MEGAcyc project,
https://github.com/lgblgblgb/megacyc.  Same author, same licence, its own
repository -- worth knowing if these timings ever need re-deriving.
