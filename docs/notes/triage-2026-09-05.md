## What the triage did not do

- Nothing was listened to.  The sequencer and the OPL2 demos are proven
  to produce signal (`seqtest`, `opltest`); whether the sequencer's noise
  channel *sounds* like a Beeb's is Doc's ear's call, and the two patches
  in `core/io.c seq_start` are the knobs.
- KEYTEST, TELNET-to-a-BBS and BENCH's full sweep need a person or a
  network and were exercised only as far as their first screen.
- The Pi build was not run (p15 unreachable today); every program here is
  host-independent guest code, so the risk is in `core/`, not in them.

Nothing to retire.  The one FIX is done.  The programs are in better shape
than the machine under them: the review's list is where the work is.
