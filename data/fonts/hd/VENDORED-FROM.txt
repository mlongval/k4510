data/fonts/hd -- the text fonts at the panel's own pixels (F12 -> Video -> Font):
at 720x540 the HD text (core/vicky.h vicky_hd_font), at 1440x1080 the machine's
own 16-wide font (K4510_FONT32_PHYS / K4510_FONT16W_PHYS).

Each face is 256 glyphs, two bytes a row, MSB first, in the K4510 code page's
order (-k4510.bin) and strict CP437's (-cp437.bin): 16x32 (<face>-*.bin) and
16x16 (<face>16-*.bin, each pair of the 16x32's rows ORed into one).  Built by
tools/mkhdfonts.py, 2026-10-06; a glyph a face lacks is unscii-16's, doubled;
the drawn and doubled faces take the line and block drawings (U+2500-259F)
from the VGA, so boxes meet.  Fetched 2026-10-06.
