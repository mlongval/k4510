/HOME -- yours.  The prompt starts here.

Documents, projects, what you write and compile.  A program of your own
in /HOME/PROJECTS/NAME/ runs by its bare name from anywhere once its
name.prg exists (PAS NAME or CC NAME makes it from NAME.PAS or NAME.C).
What programs make for you lands here too: CHESS writes its exported
games as /HOME/GAME-<date>-<time>.PGN.
