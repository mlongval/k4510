\begin{aside}
\textbf{This is alpha documentation.} It describes a machine that is
still being built, and it is written alongside it. Everything in it ---
commands, keys, registers, the layout of files, the names of things ---
is subject to change, and some of it will have changed between this
printing and the machine you are running. Where the two disagree, the
machine is right and this book is behind it; Appendix~\ref{cha:issues}
says how to tell us. The version on the cover is the build this book
was made from.
\end{aside}
