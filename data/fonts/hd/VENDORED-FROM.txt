data/fonts/hd -- the HD text fonts (core/vicky.h vicky_hd_font; F12 -> Video -> Font)

Each face is 256 glyphs of 16x32, two bytes a row, MSB first, in the K4510 code
page's order (-k4510.bin) and strict CP437's (-cp437.bin).  Built by
tools/mkhdfonts.py, 2026-10-06; a glyph a face lacks is unscii-16's, doubled.
